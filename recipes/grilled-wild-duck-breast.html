Grilled Wild Duck Breast

Description

Simple duck breast recipe. Lean and flavorful. Thighs are an optional addition to this recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1/2 tsp hot sauce of choice
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 4 skinned, boned duck breast halves(Woodduck preferred)

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Stir together Worcestershire sauce, olive oil, garlic, hot sauce, and pepper in a large bowl. Add duck breasts; toss well to coat. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator, 30 minutes to overnight.
  3. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  4. Remove duck from marinade and cook on the preheated grill to desired doneness, 5 to 7 minutes per side. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read at least 165 degrees F. Discard marinade.

Nutrition Facts

  • Calories-297
  • Fat-11g
  • Carbs-5g
  • Protein-43g
