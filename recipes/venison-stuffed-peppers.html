Venison Stuffed Peppers

Description

Stuffed peppers with ground venison are lean and flavorful at the same time. Easy and a great go to during hunting season.

Ingredients

  • 4 tsp unsalted butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 1/2 lbs ground venison
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese, divided
  • 1(6 ounce) box sage stuffing mix
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 4 large green bell peppers, halved, stems and seed removed

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a glass baking dish.
  2. Heat butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook and stir onion in hot butter until softened, 3 to 5 minutes. Set aside.
  3. Combine venison, 3/4 cup Cheddar cheese, stuffing mix, garlic powder, black pepper, and salt in a large bowl. Mix until well blended. Add enough cream to create a thick, doughy texture. Add cooked onions and mix until blended.
  4. Arrange bell pepper halves in the prepared baking dish, cut-sides up. Form meat mixture into 8 meatballs using your hands. Stuff meatballs into bell pepper halves in the baking dish.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 1 hour. Top with remaining 3/4 cup Cheddar cheese and continue baking until cheese is melted and starting to brown, about 15 minutes more.

Nutrition Facts
